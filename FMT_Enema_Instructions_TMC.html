Enema Instructions

A step-by-step guide for self-administering your FMT enema at home. Please read in full before commencing — and contact the clinic if anything is unclear.

01

Preparation & Equipment

Defrosting. Place two syringes in a container of hot tap water until warm and completely defrosted — about 30–40 minutes.

Per-treatment kit. 2 × FMT syringes, 1 × disposable catheter, 1 × disposable blue mat, 1 × sachet of lubricant.

02

Step-by-Step Administration

  1. Set up equipment. Remove the red stopper and lid from the syringes (hold upright so they don't spill). Open the catheter and lubricate the end.
  2. Lay on your left side.
  3. Slowly insert the catheter into your rectum — as high as possible. It cannot be inserted too far.
  4. Attach one syringe into the orange port at the end of the catheter.
  5. Slowly depress the syringe until empty. If there's resistance, pull the catheter out a little or re-draw. If still stuck, remove the catheter and check the holes — do not force the plunger, the syringe can pop.
  6. Fold or kink the catheter and remove the empty syringe — this stops the transplant flushing back up.
  7. Insert the second syringe and depress until empty.
  8. Remove the catheter.
  9. Remain lying down for 30 minutes minimum. Lying down ensures you can hold it in; the bacteria absorb quickly. Hold off using your bowels for as long as comfortable — there is no maximum.
  10. Dispose of all equipment in the bin. You'll get a new kit for each treatment.
03

If Something Goes Wrong

Important

If you have any difficulty administering the enema, or any concerns during or after the procedure, contact the clinic. If you experience severe pain, bleeding, or fever, stop the program and seek medical attention immediately.

In a medical emergency, call 000 immediately.
